What recurs across the work

The same questions come back in every AI product: who the user is and what they already know; what the tool may do on its own and what needs a human’s yes; what the user can see of what the model did; what happens when it fails; what evidence the tool leaves behind; and how you test any of that without a live model in the loop. OpenBots is the fullest answer so far. The smaller tools are earlier attempts at the same questions.

Lead proof: OpenBots

A native macOS app that turns Claude into a team of persistent, named teammates — durable local state, explicit approvals, Swift 6, MIT. Preview build: the executor for consequential actions is disabled.
