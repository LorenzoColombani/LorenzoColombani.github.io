# Workshop vault door — source credits

Original 2.23-second arrangement: recorded metal, servos, locks, steel travel,
pressure releases and paired end stops, plus original sub-bass/reactor/HUD accents.
Approved big-door audition, 9 October 2026. No film audio is included.

All source recordings below are offered under
[CC0 1.0](https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/):

- [servomotor.wav — vacuumfan7072](https://freesound.org/people/vacuumfan7072/sounds/264592/)
- [ratchet.wav — Nomadyag](https://freesound.org/people/Nomadyag/sounds/193058/)
- [FOLEY_Ext_Gate_Metal_001.wav — conleec](https://freesound.org/people/conleec/sounds/148630/)
- [Metal Slide 1 — LordForklift](https://freesound.org/people/LordForklift/sounds/448413/)
- [release_air_pffft.wav — reg7783](https://freesound.org/people/reg7783/sounds/131302/)

These recordings were cropped, filtered, resampled, layered and mixed with short
early reflections. The published MP3 is encoded at 48 kHz stereo / 192 kbps,
without the audition's leading or trailing silence. Source snapshots, hashes,
cue sheets, stems and the reproducible mixer are retained in the project's
private sound-reference collection.
