THE BRIDGE

Lawyers and engineers. Founders and philosophers. Humans and machines. Every specialist speaks a different language — Lorenzo Colombani translates. Certified lawyer, mediator at the Paris Court of Appeal, negotiation coach, and AI builder: born, raised, educated, and worked in multicultural environments, translating the needs of people who don't share a language so their projects ship without friction. Most people pick a lane. I build the interchange.
