Short, plain answers. Out loud.

Voice-first kit for Claude Code.

What it does

  • Software for Claude Code on a Mac. You ask out loud; Claude does the work quietly; the answer comes back short, plain and spoken. The details stay on screen.
  • A checker reads every reply before it is spoken. Too long, a table, a wall of numbers, or words the listener does not use: the reply is sent back and rewritten. You only hear the approved version.
  • An everyday-words list that grows: the moment a word loses you, you add it.

What is in the box

  • Press to talk (hold a mouse button or a foot pedal). It never listens on its own.
  • Every part has an off switch. A self-test runs at each start and once a day.
  • It speaks in the Mac’s own voice. (The ad uses a different, licensed voice.)
  • Needs: a Mac, Claude Code (its own subscription), a dictation app, jq. Setup about thirty minutes.
  • It changes how Claude talks, not what Claude knows or can do. Bad news is said as plainly as good news.
